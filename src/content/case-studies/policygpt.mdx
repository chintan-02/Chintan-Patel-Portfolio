## Problem

Enterprise policy answers need to be traceable to the documents that support them. Retrieval similarity alone cannot establish that a passage directly answers a question, and weak evidence must not produce speculative prose.

The system also needs to keep provider outages distinct from unsupported questions. Reviewers must be able to inspect document lifecycle state, page-level evidence, evaluation results and operational readiness instead of trusting an opaque chat response.

## Solution

PolicyGPT validates and registers policy PDFs with persistent identity, extracts page-aware text, creates metadata-rich chunks and indexes retrievable evidence in ChromaDB. Every question passes through an answerability gate before optional generation.

Supported results attach page-level citations and interpretable confidence diagnostics. Missing evidence produces a controlled unsupported response, while provider failure preserves answer-ready evidence through citation-only fallback. Evaluation artifacts and readiness views make quality and operational state inspectable.

It is an independent portfolio engineering project with a local Docker Compose release profile—not a generic PDF chatbot, a commercially adopted product or a cloud-production system. Intended review contexts include HR, policy ownership, compliance, governance, SOP operations and RAG engineering evaluation; they are not claims of real-company usage.

## Confidence design

Confidence is an interpretable answerability signal—not guaranteed certainty, an LLM self-rating or a claim that the generated answer is correct.

It is informed by:

- top retrieval score
- average retrieval score
- retrieval margin
- lexical coverage
- numeric consistency
- direct support
- scope risk

The final decision uses these diagnostics to explain why evidence was accepted or rejected. A high similarity score alone cannot override numeric contradictions, missing direct support or an external-authority question outside the indexed documents.

## Engineering decisions

### Why gate evidence before calling the provider?

Generation is optional and runs only after retrieved evidence passes the answerability decision. This keeps weak evidence from becoming fluent but unsupported prose.

### Why separate PostgreSQL from ChromaDB?

Document identity, lifecycle and operational metadata have different responsibilities from vector retrieval evidence. Keeping those ownership boundaries explicit makes ingestion state, duplicate prevention and recovery easier to reason about.

### Why separate provider failure from unsupported evidence?

A provider outage does not erase valid retrieved evidence. When evidence remains answer-ready, citation-only fallback preserves a useful and honest result instead of misclassifying the question as unsupported.

### Why is confidence not an LLM self-rating?

The confidence signal is derived from observable retrieval and evidence diagnostics, including strength, coverage, numeric consistency, direct support and scope risk. It does not ask a language model to grade its own answer.

## Current limitations and roadmap

PolicyGPT Enterprise is not a finished production platform. Current limitations are intentionally visible:

- no cloud deployment
- no authentication or authorization
- no tenant isolation
- synchronous ingestion
- embedded single-instance Chroma
- local source storage
- no malware scanning
- no managed secrets
- no managed backups
- no hosted monitoring
- no document deletion or reindexing
- no production usage claim

Additional production work would require TLS termination, retention and governance controls, recovery testing, broader domain validation and formal security review.

The next work is ordered by risk and operational dependency:

1. authentication, authorization and tenant isolation
2. object storage and malware scanning
3. asynchronous ingestion
4. deletion and reindexing
5. managed PostgreSQL and backups
6. telemetry and alerting
7. hybrid search and reranking
8. larger human-reviewed evaluation
9. multi-document comparison
10. compliance-report workflows
11. query routing or LangGraph only when justified

Agentic orchestration comes after identity, evaluation, observability and recovery controls—not before them.

## What this project demonstrates

I designed and implemented PolicyGPT Enterprise end to end. The project demonstrates production-style RAG architecture, evidence gating, page-level citation provenance, PostgreSQL and vector-store coordination, interpretable confidence diagnostics, safe unsupported and provider-failure fallbacks, versioned evaluation design, request-level observability and Docker Compose deployment engineering.

The strongest engineering result is the explicit system boundary: it is a verified local release profile with inspectable evidence and evaluation—not a commercial SaaS product, a cloud production deployment or proof of real-company adoption.
