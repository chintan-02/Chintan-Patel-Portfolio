## Integration conventions

- The citation-backed answer is the hero, project-preview, and social-sharing image.
- The supported and unsupported case views use a full-width stacked layout at all breakpoints so the benchmark tables remain readable.
- All non-hero screenshots lazy-load through the shared screenshot gallery.
- Every screenshot links to its original-resolution asset and preserves its intrinsic aspect ratio.
- Captions describe the verified interface state and keep the provider-disabled benchmark boundary explicit.
- The legacy captures in `public/projects/policygpt/` remain repository assets but are not displayed in the v0.3.0 case study.
